## Featured Project

### Order-to-Collection (O2C) Process Improvement

A practical example of reviewing and improving the end-to-end process from customer order through invoicing, accounts receivable, collection, and cash reporting.

**Typical workflow:**

Customer Order  
→ Commercial Terms & Approval  
→ Order Processing  
→ Shipment / Service Delivery  
→ Invoicing  
→ Accounts Receivable  
→ Collection  
→ Cash Reporting

## Key Improvement Areas

- Clear ownership at each stage
- Better handoffs between Sales, Operations, Finance, and AR
- Earlier identification of billing or collection issues
- Standardized approval and documentation requirements
- Defined exception and escalation procedures
- Improved visibility over receivables and cash collection

## Professional Approach

Many finance issues appear downstream but originate earlier in the process.

For example, an overdue receivable may not simply be an AR problem. It may result from unclear commercial terms, incorrect customer information, pricing issues, missing approvals, shipment documentation, or invoicing errors.

My approach is to identify the root cause, clarify ownership, strengthen controls, and design a process that supports both operational efficiency and financial accuracy.
